# Use versioned subledger balance projections as accounting truth

The authoritative lease-subledger state is an immutable, reproducible, self-contained Projection Version that materializes right-of-use asset cost, accumulated depreciation, current lease liability, non-current lease liability, and categorized roll-forward movements rather than depending on mutable measurement rows. Journal entries are derived posting instructions and are reconciled to the projection before the projection is reconciled to GL evidence. Current Lease Liability is the discounted principal expected to be settled in the twelve months after each reporting date, calculated after applicable remeasurements or modifications; it is not the undiscounted Maturity Analysis. Exempt short-term and low-value leases do not produce liability projection balances, and period lock designates a Frozen Projection.
